  MORE BUSINESS WITH AI — THE STARTER PROMPT PACK
  10 copy-paste prompts to win more customers and
  save hours every week. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude,
  or any AI assistant. Replace [BRACKETS] with your
  details.

1. THE 30-DAY CONTENT CALENDAR
"Act as a social media strategist for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] in
[CITY]. Create a 30-day content calendar with a hook, caption,
and call-to-action for each post. My audience is [DESCRIBE
CUSTOMER] and my goal is [GOAL]."

2. THE FOLLOW-UP THAT CLOSES
"Write a 3-email follow-up sequence for a lead who requested a
quote for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] but hasn't replied in 5 days. Keep
each email under 120 words, friendly, and end with one clear
next step."

3. THE 5-STAR REVIEW REQUEST
"Write a short, warm text message asking a happy customer to
leave a Google review after I completed [SERVICE]. Give me 3
variations with different tones."

4. THE PRICING OBJECTION HANDLER
"A customer says '[OBJECTION ABOUT PRICE]'. Give me 3 confident,
non-pushy responses that reframe the value of [PRODUCT/SERVICE]
without discounting."

5. THE IRRESISTIBLE OFFER
"Brainstorm 10 limited-time offers for my [TYPE OF BUSINESS]
that would attract new customers without training them to only
buy on discount. Explain the psychology behind each."

6. THE JOB-POST TO A-PLAYERS
"Write a job posting for a [ROLE] at my [TYPE OF BUSINESS] that
filters out the wrong applicants and excites the right ones.
Include a short screening question."

7. THE WEEKLY NEWSLETTER IN 10 MINUTES
"Turn these rough notes into a friendly customer newsletter with
a subject line and a P.S.: [PASTE NOTES]. Keep it under 250
words and sound like a real human, not a corporation."

8. THE COMPETITOR TEARDOWN
"Here is a competitor's website copy: [PASTE]. Identify 5 gaps
or weaknesses I can position against, and suggest headlines that
make my [BUSINESS] the obvious choice."

9. THE SALES CALL PREP SHEET
"I have a call with [PROSPECT / COMPANY]. Based on this info
[PASTE], give me: their likely pain points, 5 discovery
questions to ask, and 2 objections to prepare for."

10. THE 'SAVE ME HOURS' SOP
"Write a clear step-by-step standard operating procedure a new
hire could follow to [TASK]. Number each step and flag the 2
places people most often make mistakes."
